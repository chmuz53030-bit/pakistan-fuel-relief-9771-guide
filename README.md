# Pakistan 9771 Fuel Relief Scheme: A Simple Guide

This guide explains Pakistan's **9771 fuel relief scheme** in simple English: what the scheme is, who can get help, how to register by SMS, and answers to the questions people ask most often.

## What is the 9771 fuel relief scheme?

The 9771 scheme is a government relief program that gives financial help to low-income families to deal with rising fuel and transport costs. Eligible households receive a cash subsidy that can be used toward fuel expenses, reducing the pressure of high petrol prices on daily life.

The scheme works through a simple SMS-based registration system, so even people without smartphones or internet access can apply using a basic mobile phone.

## Who is eligible?

Eligibility is generally aimed at households that need support the most. The typical criteria include:

- **Low monthly household income.** The scheme targets families below a set income threshold announced with each phase of the program.
- **Valid CNIC.** You must have a valid Computerized National Identity Card. The CNIC is used to verify your identity and prevent duplicate registrations.
- **One registration per family.** Usually only one person per household can register, using their own CNIC and mobile number.
- **Registered mobile SIM.** The SIM you send the SMS from should ideally be registered in your own name, as replies and verification messages go to that number.

> Note: exact income limits and eligibility rules can change between phases. Always check the latest official announcement before applying.

## How to register by SMS: step by step

Registration is done entirely by text message. Here is the process:

1. **Open the SMS app** on your mobile phone.
2. **Type your 13-digit CNIC number** with no dashes or spaces (for example, `3520212345678`).
3. **Send the SMS to 9771.**
4. **Wait for the reply.** You will receive a confirmation message saying your application has been received.
5. **Wait for the eligibility result.** After verification against government records, you will get another SMS telling you whether you qualify and what to do next.
6. **Follow the instructions in the eligibility SMS** to receive the subsidy, which is usually disbursed through designated payment centers or partner banks.

### Tips for a successful registration

- Double-check your CNIC digits before sending — a single wrong digit means your application cannot be verified.
- Send the SMS from your own number so you receive the replies directly.
- Do not send the SMS multiple times; repeated messages do not speed up the process.
- Keep the confirmation SMS saved on your phone as proof of application.

## What happens after you register?

1. **Verification.** Your CNIC is checked against national databases (such as NADRA records and income/poverty-score data).
2. **Eligibility SMS.** You are informed whether you qualify.
3. **Disbursement.** Eligible applicants are told where and how to collect the fuel subsidy amount.
4. **Collection.** Visit the assigned center with your original CNIC to receive the payment.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there any registration fee?**
No. Registration by SMS to 9771 is free of any agent charges. Never pay anyone who claims they can "guarantee" your registration.

**I sent my CNIC but got no reply. What should I do?**
First, confirm the SMS was actually delivered (check your sent folder and balance). Network delays can take a few hours. If there is still no reply after a day, re-send once, making sure the number 9771 is correct.

**Can I register if my SIM is not in my name?**
It is strongly recommended to use a SIM registered in your own name, because verification messages and disbursement details are tied to that number.

**I was declared ineligible but I think I qualify. Can I appeal?**
Eligibility is decided from official records. If your circumstances have changed (for example, your income data is outdated), update your records with the relevant authority and watch for announcements about re-verification or new registration phases.

**How will I know this is not a scam?**
The real scheme only uses the official short code 9771 and official announcements. Be cautious of anyone asking for money, bank details, or OTP codes in exchange for "registration help" — those are scams.

**When will I receive the money?**
Disbursement timelines are announced phase by phase. Keep your phone switched on and watch for the official SMS with collection instructions.
